# Northstar — Product Vision

## Core idea
Doom Scroll Clock is a self-aware, slightly sarcastic witness to your scrolling habit. It doesn't lecture or block — it just holds up a mirror and tells you exactly how much of your finite life you've spent in the feed. Then it makes a joke about it.

## Design principles

1. **Ambient, not intrusive.** The overlay pill appears and disappears automatically. It never blocks content or demands attention.

2. **Data as comedy.** Every stat is a fun-fact comparison. You didn't waste 22 minutes — you scrolled the equivalent of Bohemian Rhapsody played twice.

3. **Honesty without shame.** The level names ("Doom Scroll Pro", "Today's MVP") are affectionate, not punishing. The app is on your side.

4. **Zero friction.** No login, no account, no cloud sync. All data lives on-device in SharedPreferences.

## What success looks like
- The pill appears within a second of starting to scroll
- The distance shown feels *surprisingly realistic* (not "I scrolled to the moon in 10 minutes")
- Hitting a new level makes you laugh, not feel guilty
- You show the app to someone and they immediately want it
